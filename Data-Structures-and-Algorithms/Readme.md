Module I: Introduction & Sorting
Introduction to Algorithms: Definition, role of algorithms in computing, and algorithm design basics.
Analyzing Algorithms & Complexity: Asymptotic notations ($O, \Omega, \Theta$), time and space complexity, and worst-case/average-case analysis.
Growth of Functions & Performance Measurements:** Mathematical foundations of growth rates and empirical performance evaluation.
Sorting & Order Statistics:
  Shell Sort
  Quick Sort
  Merge Sort
  Heap Sort
  Comparison of Sorting Algorithms
  Sorting in Linear Time (Counting Sort, Radix Sort, Bucket Sort)

Module II: Advanced Data Structures
Balanced Search Trees: Red-Black Trees (properties, rotations, insertions, deletions).
Multiway Search Trees: B-Trees (operations and structural properties).
Advanced Heaps: 
  Binomial Heaps (structure and union operations)
  Fibonacci Heaps (ameliorated operations and amortized analysis)

Module III: Algorithm Design Paradigms (Divide & Conquer & Greedy)
Divide and Conquer:
  Methodology and recurrence relations (Master Theorem / Substitution method)
  Examples: Merge/Quick Sort, Matrix Multiplication (Strassen’s), Convex Hull, and Binary Searching.
Greedy Algorithms:
  Principles of greedy choice and optimal substructure
  Examples: Optimal Reliability Allocation, Fractional Knapsack
Graph Algorithms: Minimum Spanning Trees (Prim’s and Kruskal’s algorithms)
Shortest Paths: Single-Source Shortest Paths (Dijkstra’s and Bellman-Ford algorithms)

Module IV: Dynamic Programming, Backtracking & Branch and Bound
Dynamic Programming:
Memoization vs. Tabulation, overlapping subproblems, and optimal substructure
Examples: 0/1 Knapsack, All-Pairs Shortest Paths (Warshall’s and Floyd-Warshall algorithms), Resource Allocation Problem.*Backtracking & Branch and Bound:
Systematic search frameworks and state-space trees
Examples: Travelling Salesman Problem (TSP), Graph Coloring, N-Queen Problem, Hamiltonian Cycles, and Sum of Subsets.

Module V: Selected Advanced Topics
Algebraic Computation & Fast Fourier Transform (FFT): Polynomial multiplication and evaluation.
String Matching: Naive approach, KMP algorithm, and Rabin-Karp algorithm.
Theory of NP-Completeness: P vs. NP, polynomial-time verification, NP-hardness, and polynomial-time reductions.
Approximation Algorithms: Coping with NP-hardness through bounded error solutions.
Randomized Algorithms: Introduction to probabilistic algorithms and Vegas/Monte Carlo paradigms.
